# Criterio Support

## Getting help

For help with Criterio, bug reports, or feature requests, open an issue in this repository: [Criterio support issues](https://github.com/Criterio-app/Criterio/issues).

Please include the app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not post your comparison text, personal information, screenshots containing private information, passwords, API keys, or other sensitive material in a public issue.

## Common questions

### Why am I asked before scoring?

Scoring sends the question and options you entered to `api.typesafe.pro`, which uses TypeSafe AI to return a comparison score. The first comparison asks for your consent. You can make Criterio ask again before the next comparison in **Settings → Ask before my next comparison**.

### Do I need an account or an API key?

No. Criterio does not require sign-in and does not ask you for a personal API key. An internet connection is required for scoring.

### How do I remove my saved data?

Open **Settings → Delete all local data**, then confirm **Delete draft and comparisons**. This removes the local draft, recent comparison history, and stored sharing preference from that device. It cannot retrieve content already sent to an online scoring service.

### Why did scoring fail or take time?

The service needs an internet connection. Keep the app open, then try again. If the provider is unavailable or rate-limits a request, Criterio keeps your draft so you can retry later.
